Two BepInEx mods for Chop Chop Inc. More Wood multiplies tree, log and respawn drops, with per-prefab overrides and stack-size scaling. Chop Chop Tweaks scales axe damage, swing speed, move speed, money in and out, craft output, auto-craft speed, stamina use and stat gains, plus a minigame skip and item magnet. Everything defaults to vanilla and is tunable in-game with F1.
