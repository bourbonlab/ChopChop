[size=4][b]More Wood — drop multipliers[/b][/size]

[b]Multiply what everything drops.[/b] One multiplier covers trees dropping logs and logs dropping wood; separate multipliers cover per-hit debris and timed resource regrowth, so you can boost the drops that matter without spraying woodchips everywhere.

[b]Fractional multipliers work properly.[/b] A 1.5x on a single-item drop gives you 1 or 2 with the correct long-run average, instead of quietly rounding down to nothing.

[b]Per-prefab targeting.[/b] Restrict the whole mod to specific items by name fragment, or give individual items their own multiplier — e.g. logs x4 but wood x6 — overriding the category value.

[b]Stack size instead of object count.[/b] A separate multiplier makes one pickup [i]worth[/i] more rather than spawning more physical objects. Five trunks landing on one spawn point is five rigidbodies for the physics solver to fight over; one trunk worth five is free. This is the recommended way to get big numbers.

[b]Anti-stacking scatter.[/b] Extra objects that the game would spawn at one exact point get a small random horizontal offset, so they don't interpenetrate and rocket across the map. Adjustable, and it leaves the game's own randomisation alone where it already exists.

[b]A safety cap.[/b] A hard per-drop ceiling means a mistyped multiplier can't turn a tree into a physics bomb.

[b]Spawn logging.[/b] Turn it on to see every drop in the log as [i]LogName: 2 -> 6 (x3)[/i] — which is also how you find the prefab names to use in the targeting options.

[size=4][b]Chop Chop Tweaks — everything else[/b][/size]

[b]Axe damage.[/b] Fewer swings per tree. Scales damage only — healing is never touched.

[b]Swing speed.[/b] A real attack speed: windup, hit and recovery all scale together, and the first-person hands follow correctly. Applies from your very next swing, no reload.

[b]Movement speed.[/b] Separate walk and run multipliers. The game derives ground acceleration from the ratio between them, so scaling both together keeps the vanilla feel. [i](These two apply on the next save load rather than instantly.)[/i]

[b]Money, both directions.[/b] Income and cost are separate multipliers, so you can make selling more generous without also making the shop free — or set cost to 0 and make it free on purpose.

[b]Skip the crafting minigame.[/b] Crafts complete the instant you pick a recipe. Ingredients are still required and still consumed; it goes through the game's own craft call, so nothing is bypassed except the minigame itself.

[b]Craft output multiplier.[/b] More output per craft for the same ingredient cost — the recipe-efficiency knob. Like the stack multiplier above, it grows stack size rather than object count, with its own safety cap.

[b]Automated crafting speed.[/b] Speeds up automated crafters (hand crafting has no timer). It composes with the game's own CraftSpeed upgrades rather than overwriting them, so buying an upgrade still helps.

[b]Stamina use multiplier.[/b] Scales how much stamina everything costs — set it to 0 and the bar never moves. Regeneration is left at the vanilla rate on purpose: draining and refilling half as fast would just cancel out.

[b]Stat gain multiplier.[/b] Multiplies the permanent stat points you earn from the treadmill, the weight bench and mission rewards, so a single workout is worth several. Temporary food and drink buffs are left alone, and the game's own hard cap still applies — a big multiplier reaches the ceiling sooner rather than passing through it.

[b]Item magnet.[/b] Optional, off by default. Set a radius in metres and nearby items come to you. It collects through the game's normal pickup path, so it can never grab something you couldn't have picked up by walking into it, and there's a toggle for whether it should also take items meant for deliberate pickup.

[size=4][b]Design notes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Vanilla by default.[/b] Every value starts where the game left it.
[*][b]Save-safe.[/b] Multipliers are applied to values in flight and never written into save data, shared recipe assets or shared prefab tables — so nothing compounds over time and nothing persists if you uninstall.
[*][b]Live editing.[/b] Everything except the two movement multipliers takes effect immediately.
[*][b]Use one or both.[/b] The two plugins are independent.
[/list]
